Informed Consent

TREATMENT TO BE DONE: I understand and consent to have any treatment done by the dentist after the procedure, risk, benefits & cost have been fully explained. These include, but are not limited to, X-rays, cleaning, periodontal treatments, fillings, crowns, bridges, all types of extraction, root canals, and/or dentures, local anesthetics & surgical cases.

DRUGS & MEDICATIONS: I understand that antibiotics, analgesics, & other medications can cause allergic reactions like redness & swelling of tissue, pain, itching, vomiting, and/or anaphylactic shock.

CHANGES IN TREATMENT PLAN: I understand that during treatment, it may be necessary to change/add procedures because of conditions found while working on the teeth that were not discovered during examination.

RADIOGRAPH: I understand that an x-ray or radiograph may be necessary as part of diagnostic aid, but that will not give a 100% assurance for the accuracy of the treatment.

REMOVAL OF TEETH: I understand alternatives to tooth removal exist and I authorize the dentist to proceed after these have been explained to me.

CROWNS (CAPS) & BRIDGES: Preparing a tooth may irritate the nerve tissue, leaving it sensitive to heat, cold & pressure.

ENDODONTICS (ROOT CANAL): There is no guarantee that a root canal treatment will save a tooth.

PERIODONTAL DISEASE: This is a serious condition causing gum & bone inflammation and/or loss.

FILLINGS: Care must be exercised in chewing on a new filling, especially during the first 24 hours.

DENTURES: Wearing dentures can be difficult at first — sore spots, altered speech & difficulty eating are common.

I understand that dentistry is not an exact science and that no dentist can properly guarantee accurate results all the time.
