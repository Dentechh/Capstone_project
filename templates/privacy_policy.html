Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ last_updated }}

Capizonda Dental Clinic ("we," "us," or "our") operates this website and patient portal to let patients book appointments, manage their dental records, and pay for services online. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (or your Google account details if you sign in with Google).
  • Patient intake information: address, contact number, birthday, age, sex, nationality, religion, occupation, and civil status, collected when you book an appointment.
  • Medical history: answers to our health questionnaire (current treatments, past surgeries/hospitalizations, medications, allergies, tobacco use, and, for women, pregnancy/nursing/birth-control status), and your signed consent form.
  • Treatment records: procedures performed, dentist notes, dental chart images, prescribed medicine, and billing/payment history.
  • Payment information: when you pay via GCash, your payment is processed by our third-party payment processor, PayMongo. We do not store your GCash credentials or full payment card details ourselves.
  • Profile picture: if you choose to upload one.
  • Technical information: session/login data needed to keep you signed in.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your patient account and appointment bookings.
  • To let our dentist review your medical history and provide safe, appropriate treatment.
  • To maintain your dental chart and treatment/payment records over time.
  • To process payments for services rendered.
  • To send you appointment confirmation, acceptance, or decline notifications by email.
  • To detect and prevent duplicate patient records and scheduling conflicts.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal or medical information. We share limited data only with:

  • Google LLC — if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Google Firebase/Firestore — our secure database and authentication provider, which stores your account and records.
  • PayMongo — to process GCash payments.
  • Our clinic's dentist and authorized administrative staff, who need access to your records to provide care.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our patients or staff.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase's secure cloud infrastructure. Access to the admin dashboard, which contains patient medical and financial records, is restricted to authorized clinic staff and protected by login credentials and session timeouts. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no online system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is processed.
  • Access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information.
  • Object to certain uses of your data.
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

6. Data Retention

We retain patient and treatment records for as long as your account is active or as needed to comply with applicable dental/medical record-keeping regulations, whichever is longer.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for use by adults booking on behalf of themselves or their dependents. Parents or guardians are responsible for information submitted on behalf of a minor patient.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
